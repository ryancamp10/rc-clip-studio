Privacy Policy

RC Clip Studio · last updated October 2, 2026

RC Clip Studio ("the app") is a private desktop tool used only by its owners to manage their own YouTube channel, Money Talk Clips. This policy explains what Google and YouTube data the app accesses and what it does with that data.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What data the app accesses

  • Your Google sign-in token, granted when you sign in with Google. It lets the app act on your own YouTube channel.
  • Your channel's uploads: video titles, links and public view counts.
  • Videos you choose to upload, with their titles, descriptions and settings.

How the data is used

  • To upload clips to your own channel, with the title, description, paid-promotion disclosure and "not made for kids" setting you approved.
  • To post a comment on your own uploads.
  • To match your uploads to the app's posting log and to show their view counts.

The app uses the data only for these purposes. It is not used for advertising, it is not sold, and it is not used to build profiles of anyone.

Where the data is stored

Everything is stored only on the owners' own computer: the sign-in token in a private folder, and video links and view counts in local files. The app has no server and no online database. Its AI features run locally on the same computer.

Sharing

The app does not share Google or YouTube data with anyone. Data is only sent to Google and YouTube themselves, through their official APIs, to carry out the actions above.

Cookies and tracking

The app does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. This website does not either.

Removing access and deleting data

  • You can remove the app's access at any time on your Google Account's third-party access page.
  • Deleting the app's local data folder removes the stored sign-in token and all saved links and view counts.
  • Data the app has fetched from the YouTube API is refreshed regularly and is not kept longer than needed for the purposes above.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
