RC Clip Studio

A private desktop tool for publishing short clips to our own YouTube channel.

RC Clip Studio is a small, private app that runs on our own computer. It is used only by its two owners to manage the Money Talk Clips YouTube channel. It is not offered to the public, and nobody else can sign in to it.

What it does

  • Cuts short vertical clips from source videos that campaigns we have joined provide to us, with permission to clip them.
  • Uploads those clips to our own YouTube channel, with a title, a description and a paid-promotion disclosure, after one of us approves each post.
  • Posts a comment on our own uploads.
  • Reads the public view counts of our own videos so we can see which clips perform best.

How it uses Google sign-in

The app uses Google's official sign-in and the YouTube Data API. It only acts on the channel of the account that signed in, and only for the actions listed above. See the Privacy Policy for full details.
